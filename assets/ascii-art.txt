HOW TO USE IN EMAIL
===================

1. Wrap ASCII art in <pre> tags
2. Use monospace font: 'Courier New', Courier, monospace
3. Set appropriate color (green for terminal, blue for dividers)
4. Keep line-height: 1 or 1.2 to prevent spacing issues

Example:
<pre style="margin: 0; color: #00ff00; font-family: 'Courier New', monospace; line-height: 1;">
YOUR ASCII ART HERE
</pre>

Note: Some email clients may render characters differently.
Test on Outlook, iOS Mail, and Android before sending!
